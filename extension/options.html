EE71 Monitor

An unofficial monitor for the Alcatel EE71 mobile router.

Version —

Router status

Battery, mobile network, signal, connection and Wi-Fi clients.

Battery alerts

Configurable low-battery and one-time full-charge notifications.

Local and private

No cloud, analytics, advertising or transmission to third parties.

How data is accessed

The extension reads system information exposed by the router home page without signing in to the administration panel. It does not change router settings.
